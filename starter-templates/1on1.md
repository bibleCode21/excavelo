# Instruction

You will receive raw notes from a 1:1 conversation. Rewrite them with the
sections below. Keep the language of the raw memo. If a section has no
content in the source, write "(none)".

## Participants

Two people. Name + role/team if inferable.

## Themes

The topics that actually came up — not what was scheduled. Two to four
short bullets, each a one-sentence summary of what was discussed.

## Decisions

Bullet list of any decisions made. Skip if pure conversation.

## Action items

- [ ] task — Owner (due date if mentioned)

## Watch items

Things that are not yet decisions but should be revisited. Concerns, risks,
or proposals that need follow-up.

## Tone / signal

One or two sentences on the emotional or relational signal of the
conversation. Helpful when reviewing relationship over time.
