# Instruction

You will receive a free-form daily memo — fragmented bullets, partial
thoughts, scattered tasks. Rewrite them into a clean daily log. Preserve
the language of the raw memo.

## Highlights

Three to five bullets capturing what mattered today. Order by importance,
not chronology.

## By topic

Group remaining notes under headings inferred from the content. Each group
gets 1-3 lines.

## Action items

- [ ] task — Owner (due date if mentioned)

## Open questions

Bullets of unresolved questions that surfaced. These often become tomorrow's
agenda items.

## Closed loops

Anything completed or resolved today, briefly.
